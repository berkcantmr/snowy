## What it does

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="33%" valign="top">
      <h3>❄️ Weather on your desktop</h3>
      Snow, heavy snow, rain, fireflies, cherry blossoms, autumn leaves and falling stars —
      drawn over the desktop or behind your windows. Follow the seasons, change at random,
      or match the real weather outside.
    </td>
    <td width="33%" valign="top">
      <h3>⛄ Snow you can play with</h3>
      Snow piles up on window edges and the taskbar. Roll snowballs with your mouse, throw
      them, and stack them into snowmen you can name and dress up.
    </td>
    <td width="33%" valign="top">
      <h3>🎄 Decorate</h3>
      Grow a Christmas tree and trim it, hang string lights across the screen, send the
      snowplough when the snow gets deep — and keep an eye out for the Yeti and Santa.
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="33%" valign="top">
      <h3>🏔️ 4K winter wallpapers</h3>
      Six winter photo themes, each with a day, sunset and night picture that follow the
      time of day.
    </td>
    <td width="33%" valign="top">
      <h3>🤝 Friends</h3>
      Optional: add friends by code, throw snowballs and send gifts to each other, or share
      a desktop and decorate it together. End-to-end encrypted.
    </td>
    <td width="33%" valign="top">
      <h3>🪶 Light</h3>
      Drawn on the GPU, pauses on battery and in fullscreen games, works on every monitor.
      Everything is one click away in the tray.
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>

## Get Snowy

Snowy is available **only from the Microsoft Store**:
**[apps.microsoft.com/detail/9N8TBP63JS3X](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9N8TBP63JS3X)**.
The Store installs it, keeps it up to date and removes it cleanly.

> Using an older version installed from GitHub (0.1.x)? It keeps working, but it will not
> receive updates any more. Uninstall it from *Settings › Apps*, then install Snowy from the
> Store — your snowmen, decorations and settings carry over.
