## What's new

- **Snow drifts in the wind.** Piled-up snow on the taskbar and on windows now forms hills and dips instead of a flat layer, and the wind slowly pushes the drifts along.
- **Maximum snow depth.** Settings > Desktop > "Maximum snow depth" sets how high snow can pile up, from 30 % to 300 % of the default.
- **Smooth decorations with the weather off.** Snowmen, trees and lights no longer stutter at a few frames per second while the weather is paused or turned off.
- **What's new catches you up.** If you skipped a version, the "What's new" window now also shows what came in the versions you missed.

## Install

Download `Snowy-0.1.5-setup.exe`, or use the portable zip. Installed copies offer this update from the tray menu. The `.sha256` file next to the installer holds its checksum.
