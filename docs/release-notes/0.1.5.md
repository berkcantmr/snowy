## What's new

- **Snow drifts in the wind.** Piled-up snow on the taskbar and on windows now forms hills and dips instead of a flat layer, and the wind slowly pushes the drifts along.
- **Maximum snow depth.** Settings > Desktop > "Maximum snow depth" sets how high snow can pile up, from 30 % to 300 % of the default. Changing it doesn't resize the snow already there: it grows with the snowfall, or melts down over a few seconds.
- **Snowballs sit in the snow.** A resting snowball sinks slightly into the snow, and flakes slide off it and pile up beside it instead of falling through.
- **Smooth decorations with the weather off.** Snowmen, trees and lights no longer stutter at a few frames per second while the weather is paused or turned off.
- **What's new catches you up.** If you skipped a version, the "What's new" window now also shows what came in the versions you missed.

## Fixes

- **Updates no longer roll back.** Snowy now quits properly when the installer asks it to close, and the installer makes sure Snowy is closed before replacing it. Before, an in-app update could fail and leave Snowy running without snow and without its shortcut.
- **One thing per drag.** Dragging a snowball in front of a tree no longer drags the tree too, and dragging a tree near the snow no longer starts a snowball.
- **Editors come to the front.** Clicking a decoration opens its editor in front of other windows, and it stays on top until you close it.

## Install

Download `Snowy-0.1.5-setup.exe`, or use the portable zip. Installed copies offer this update from the tray menu. The `.sha256` file next to the installer holds its checksum.
